I see her here alone but for her pooch.
She must have seen me here alone but for my thoughts.
We exchange smiles awkwardly across the coffee shop from our separate tables.
I will always pat the dog as I go past. Her dog like me. The small talk is forced, but always consistent and unique.
Today she had been to the beach, her dog likes people for the pats and attention.
I see her alone again today, I pat the dog. My mind plays over introducing myself. The pointlessness of  being alone. I rehurse all the lines, but no replies. I am foolish and a creep. What could she see in me. I’ve been alone so long now a polite smile from a stranger feels like affection. I am starving, in a desert I see an oasis but it is a mirage, I find a drop of water and it’s a pale. I am doomed to walk this desert of drips and mirages not knowing right from wrong left from right. But I think what if she is in her own desert, and feeling the same. Al we need to do is connect, talk possibly touch. I look over again and pretend my attention is on her dog. I smile not from the dog, but the warmth that strangers smile brings me.
I finish my coffee and leave nothing said, nothing gained. I’ll spare her the train wreck and embarrassment that is me.
