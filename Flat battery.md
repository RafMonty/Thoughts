Standing looking out at the rolling turquoise sea.
I can hear it crashing somewhere beneath me.
I’ve stood there some time now, fixating on a lower ledge.
Just climb the fence and shimmy down.
Out of sight from preying eyes.
The gulls have started to gather and chat.
I’m unsure what side their words of encouragement lean.
They put on their show, squabbling and bickering, placing bets, I presume.
The wind carries the spray even at this height.
I dare not move; the small outcrop now has my full attention. 
This sick joke of a life with all its calamity. 
Where if we’re I more mentally feeble one would swear this is all at the whim of some greater sociopath.
I’m sure some gulls lost their wagers as I turn and seek refuge in my warm car.
I walk back slowly with the ledge firmly burned into my mind. Back to the car. 
Go to start the engine and put the heater on.
Fucking flat battery c’mon.
