I try so hard to make your life go right.
Put everything before myself.
Others tell me to let you go, colleagues, experts in their various fields.
Just like your mum did when she cut you loose.
I wish I had her resolve, but for me, the tether is stronger and forms a noose.
Years of conditioning, resistance have etched it deep.
But I still can’t cut this damn noose so all I can do is lay low, do what I can to take the bumps out of your road.
But every loud bang, yell of your voice, frustrated pacing, I want  to kill myself, because it highlights that I failed you and I know there is no escaping this cycle.
While I am alive.
